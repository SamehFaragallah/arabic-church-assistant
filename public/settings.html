End of a sentenceHow long a pause ends a sentence

        

          

          
Shorter: verses appear sooner. Longer: fewer sentences cut in two by a breath. 0.5 s suits most preachers.

        

      

      

        
Voice sensitivityHow easily speech is picked up

        

          

          
Raise it if quiet speech is missed; lower it if music or the congregation is taken for the pastor.

Always kept on this PC: every sentence heard, every verse found, everything put on screen with its time and who or what put it
            there (History tab, data\history), and the app's and speech engine's log (data\logs, 60 days).

Detection

      

        
Auto-showPut spoken references on screen without clicking Show

        

      

      

        
Follow the readingThe pastor reads on into the next verse

        

          
          
Works from the words themselves, so it follows a passage read straight through without anyone saying “the next verse”.
            When one sentence runs across two verses both are shown, so no verse is skipped.

          
          
In a passage that was announced, or after three verses in a row, the next verse goes up as the one on screen ends, so the screen keeps
            pace with the reader instead of trailing a verse behind. If he stops reading there, the screen goes back to the verse he ended on. At the last verse of a chapter the screen stays: it does not go on into the next chapter by itself.

          

          
How long before the end of a verse the next one goes up. At 0 it waits for the verse's last word; at 1 second it goes up about
            two words earlier, while the last words are still being read.

        

      

      

        
Follow “next verse”«العدد اللي بعده» · «اللي بعده» · “next verse”

        

          
          
A plain «اللي بعده» only counts while you are reading scripture, and not in sentences like «اليوم اللي بعده».

        

      

      

        
Catch quotesSuggest verses quoted without naming them

        

      

      

        
Auto-show quotesVerses recognized from a quote, Arabic or English

        

          
          
Off by default: a quote is recognized from a few words, so now and then it can be the wrong verse. Only clear quotes go on screen by themselves: about six words of the verse in a row, or five when hardly another verse has them («الرب راعي فلا يعوزني شيء»). A verse that is quoted again after it has left the screen comes back.

          
          
On: a clear quote goes up as soon as enough of the verse has been said (about six words), while the speaker is still in it. Off: it goes up when he finishes the sentence. Only for quotes that go on screen by themselves (the switch above).

          

        

      

      

        
Set prayersWords said in every service

        

          
A formula or prayer that is said every week is not a quotation: «باسم الآب والابن والروح القدس» should not bring up Matthew 28:19.
            List them here. Each is known by its first five words (small slips in spelling are allowed).

          

          

            
            
            
            
          

          
Ignore: its words are left out when quotes are looked for. Card only: a card for its verse, never on screen by itself.
            Show passage: its passage goes on screen like a clear quote (when Auto-show quotes is on).

PlaylistsThe video titles are saved on this PC

        

          

            
            
          

          

          
When the speaker says one of the titles («هنرنم ترنيمة …»), the video shows up highlighted in the console, ready to play on the display.

        

      

      

        
Detect titlesListen for the playlist titles

        

      

      

        
Play automaticallyOff by default

        

          
          
It plays by itself only when the speaker says that we are about to sing or hear it: «خلونا نرنم مع بعض ترنيمة …», «تعالوا نسبح ونقول …», «نسمع ترنيمة …», “let's sing …”.
            A first line said in a prayer, a title on its own, or a hymn quoted in the sermon («فيه ترنيمة بتقول …») only makes a card with a Play button. A hymn that is announced but is not in your
            playlists never plays by itself: its card lists what the song database (Videos tab) has by that name.

          
          
When its title is mentioned again later in the service, its card comes to the top and Play starts it. Turn this off to have it play
            every time it is announced. It starts afresh each time Arabic Church Assistant is opened.

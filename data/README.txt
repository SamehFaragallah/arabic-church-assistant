This folder holds what Arabic Church Assistant uses and keeps:

- The Bibles (Arabic Van Dyke with and without tashkeel; English KJV, BSB, WEB and ASV), the fonts for the display,
  and the song list (songs.json). These come with the app.
- What it keeps of each service on this PC: history, logs, recordings, the service plan and the playlists' videos.

Before giving a copy of the app's folder to another church, delete history, logs and recordings (the record of your
services), and settings.json in the main folder if you do not want to pass on your settings, keys and phone PIN.
